Active Builds

BikeDash Field Test C · Kotlin

Android bike computer with a C core (NDK) under a thin Kotlin shell. It rides your music's volume up and down with GPS speed — audible over wind, not blasting at stoplights — and synthesizes nine procedural vehicle soundscapes in C (truck, horse, boat, prop plane, starship…) with hold-to-rev and sensor-reactive one-shots. Offline vector maps (12 US regions in under a megabyte), waypoints, ghost race, theft alarm, battery telemetry. Battery-first: the premium build is ≈2 MB with zero network permissions. 480+ unit tests across the C core and JVM.

repo: private while it's on the bike
Open Survey Drone Design Phase HW · C · Py

An open-source, low-cost multi-sensor survey drone. Three LiDAR ranging heads on servo pan mounts (forward, aft, down), filter-modified NoIR cameras for multispectral NDVI, and an RGB camera for the pilot. Digital FPV pushes live video with telemetry overlay to AR display glasses at roughly 52 ms glass-to-glass; control is standard ExpressLRS RC. The sensor package rides a custom Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W carrier PCB — Eagle layout, opto-isolated, regulated straight off the flight LiPo — replacing a breadboard that weighed too much to fly.

hardware + docs publish as it matures
