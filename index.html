01 / Services

What I do.

Six things. If yours isn't listed, ask anyway.

  1. 01

    3D printing

    Parts printed to order on a QIDI Max 4. The bed is 390 × 390 × 340 mm, so brackets, enclosures, and replacement parts too big for a desktop printer come out here in one piece. Bring an STL, a broken original to measure, or a sketch on paper, and I'll model it first.

    PETG by default· Other materials on request· One-offs and small runs

  2. 02

    Computer builds & repair

    Custom desktops built to a budget, upgrades, and component-level repair. Diagnosis on the bench, then a straight answer on whether it's worth fixing. When it isn't, I usually have a refurbished machine that is.

    Builds· Upgrades· Repairs· Refurbished machines

  3. 03

    IT support

    Sixteen years of desktop support in universities and small companies: Windows and Linux, printers, home networks, backups, malware cleanup, everything moved over to the new machine. In person around Greater Portland, or remote when that's enough.

    Homes and small offices· Setup and migration· Backups· Networks

  4. 04

    Bicycle repair

    Tune-ups, flats, brakes, shifting, and full overhauls of the bike that has been in the garage since the move. I ride every day and fix my own; yours gets the same care.

    Tune-ups· Brakes and shifting· Flats· Overhauls

  5. 05

    Drone photography

    Aerial photographs and video of houses, land, roofs, and events. Also the mapping side: orthomosaics and elevation models built from drone imagery, with GIS deliverables if you need them.

    Stills· Video· Roof and site views· Orthomosaic maps

  6. 06

    Used goods

    Refurbished computers and parts, bikes and bike parts, and the surplus a shop like this collects. Tested before it's listed. The current shelf is below.
